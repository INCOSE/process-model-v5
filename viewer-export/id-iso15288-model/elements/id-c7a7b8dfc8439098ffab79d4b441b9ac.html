Supply response ()
Prepared by a potential supplier to support the offer of a contract bid, including cost, schedule, risk statements, methodology to satisfy the request for supply, experiences and capabilities, any recommendations to tailor the request for supply or contract, and the signature of the supplier's approving authority. Informally, may be prepared within an organization. (Adapted from ISO/IEC/IEEE 15289, 2019)
