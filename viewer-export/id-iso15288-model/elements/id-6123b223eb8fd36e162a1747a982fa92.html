Portfolio Management ()
As stated in ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023, [6.2.3.1] The purpose of the Portfolio Management process is to initiate and sustain necessary, sufficient and suitable projects in order to meet the strategic objectives of the organization. See detailed description in the INCOSE Handbook v.5, page 57
