Infrastructure Management ()
As stated in ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023, [6.2.2.1] The purpose of the Infrastructure Management process is to provide the infrastructure and services to projects to support organization and project objectives throughout the life cycle. See detailed description in the INCOSE Handbook v.5, page 54
