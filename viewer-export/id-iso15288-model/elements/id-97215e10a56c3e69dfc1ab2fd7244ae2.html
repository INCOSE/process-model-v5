Human Resource Management ()
As stated in ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023, [6.2.4.1] The purpose of the Human Resource Management process is to provide the organization with necessary human resources and to maintain their competencies, consistent with business needs. See detailed description in the INCOSE Handbook v.5, page 60
